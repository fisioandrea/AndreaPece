My Path: From Data to the Finish Line

Event Manager & Lecturer at University of Rome Tor Vergata

My journey to becoming an event manager is unconventional, rooted in a passion for precision and a deep-seated desire to create exceptional experiences. I started my career as a researcher, where I honed my skills in data analysis and meticulous attention to detail.

Later, working in a hospital environment, I developed my ability to remain calm under pressure, make critical decisions, and lead teams through complex and high-stakes situations. This unique background is the core of my approach. I don't just follow blueprints; I use data-driven insights and my knack for innovative problem-solving to redefine what's possible in event management.

I am a leader who empowers my team, a strategist who sees beyond the logistics, and a creator who is always searching for the next groundbreaking idea. My goal is to transform every challenge into an opportunity for innovation and every event into a lasting memory.
